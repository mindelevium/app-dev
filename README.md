# Breaking Bad 💚

## About the Series
Breaking Bad is an American crime drama television series created by Vince Gilligan. It originally aired from 2008 to 2013 and follows the story of Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher who gets diagnosed with lung cancer and turns to producing and selling methamphetamine to secure his family's financial future.

## Basic Information
- **Title:** Breaking Bad
- **Creator:** Vince Gilligan
- **Genre:** Crime, Drama, Thriller
- **Release Date:** January 20, 2008
- **Final Episode:** September 29, 2013
- **Seasons:** 5
- **Total Episodes:** 62
- **Original Network:** AMC
- **Country:** United States

## Main Characters
- **Walter White** – A chemistry teacher who becomes involved in the drug business.
- **Jesse Pinkman** – Walter's former student and business partner.
- **Skyler White** – Walter's wife who becomes suspicious of his activities.
- **Hank Schrader** – A DEA agent and Walter's brother-in-law.
- **Saul Goodman** – A criminal lawyer who helps Walter and Jesse.
- **Gustavo Fring** – A powerful drug distributor.

## Story Overview
The series follows Walter White as he enters the illegal drug trade after learning about his cancer diagnosis. Together with Jesse Pinkman, he starts producing methamphetamine. As the story progresses, Walter becomes more powerful and gets involved in dangerous situations that affect his family, relationships, and personal life.

## Main Themes
- Crime and consequences
- Power and greed
- Family and responsibility
- Identity and transformation
- Morality and corruption
